--- INDIGENOUS LANGUAGE SCRIPT ---
Gaisuwa ta musamman ga dukkan makiyayan mu! Bayanan yanayi na AgriIntel sun nuna cewa ma'aunin zafi da lema (THI) zai haura tamanin (80). Wannan yana nufin dabbobi za su shiga cikin tsananin kunci na zafi. Don kiyaye nono da lafiyar shanunku: Ku sauya lokacin kiwo zuwa sanyin safiya da kuma yamma bayan la'asar. A tsakiyar rana tsakanin karfe goma sha biyu zuwa uku, ku tsayar da shanu a karkashin inuwar bishiyoyi. Ku tabbatar suna samun ruwan sha mai sanyi da gishirin lasa. Wannan zai hana zazzabin zafi da rage yawan nono.

--- ENGLISH EQUIVALENT ---
Warm greetings to all pastoralists! AgriIntel meteorological data indicates the Temperature-Humidity Index (THI) will exceed 80. This triggers severe heat stress in livestock. To preserve milk yield and herd vitality: Shift grazing schedules to early mornings and late afternoons. During peak solar irradiance between 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM, hold cattle under dense tree shade. Ensure continuous access to cool drinking water and mineral salt licks to prevent thermal exhaustion.
