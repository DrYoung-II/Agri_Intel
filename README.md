# 🌱 AgriIntel — The Generative Intelligence Platform for Agriculture
> **Africa-First, Globally Relevant: The Intelligence Infrastructure Behind Resilient Agriculture**

---

## 📖 Executive Summary & Founder Story
> *"I trained as an animal geneticist, spent years working with agricultural research problems, transitioned into data science and artificial intelligence, and realised that one of Africa's biggest agricultural problems is not merely lack of knowledge—it is the inability to connect agricultural knowledge, data, research and decision-making at scale."*

While generic agricultural AI applications stop at *"upload a photo of your tomato leaf and I'll identify the disease"*, **AgriIntel** builds the underlying intelligence infrastructure. It connects genomics, biometrical field trials, edaphic soil chemistry, climate projections, farm economics, and macro-policy simulations to generate actionable, evidence-grounded knowledge for farmers, researchers, students, agribusinesses, and governments.

## 💡 Strategic Assessment: Novelty & Market Scalability

### 1. How Novel is This Project?
- **Current Landscape**: 95% of agricultural AI applications deployed in Africa focus strictly on basic farmer-facing diagnostics. While useful, they address symptoms rather than agricultural systems, and fail researchers, breeders, policy analysts, and commercial agribusinesses.
- **The AgriIntel Moat**: AgriIntel moves upstream to the **intelligence infrastructure** of agriculture. By connecting:
  1. **Quantitative Genetics & Biometrics** (Pedigree/genomic BLUP, mixed-effects models, Duncan's Multiple Range Test, ANOVA),
  2. **Evidence-Grounded Knowledge Graphs** (linking Gene $\rightarrow$ Trait $\rightarrow$ Breed $\rightarrow$ Soil $\rightarrow$ Climate $\rightarrow$ Economic Outcome),
  3. **Institutional Macro-Policy & Economic Simulators** (multi-ministerial fiscal impact, 12-commodity import substitution balance sheets, macro-shock resilience, and AU CAADP/FEC cabinet policy briefs), and
  4. **Multi-Agent Orchestration** (Climate, Crop, Genetics, Soil, Economics, and Evidence Agents collaborating on a single inquiry),
  AgriIntel creates a **systemic decision engine** with no direct equivalent in the African or global tech ecosystem.

### 2. Can It Reach Millions of Users?
**Yes, through a 4-Tier Flywheel:**
1. **Tier 1: University Students, Lecturers, & Researchers (2–3M users)**
   - Over 500+ agricultural faculties across Africa and the Global South.
   - Core value: AI Research Scientist, Thesis Tutor, and automated Experimental Design & Statistical Analysis (saving hundreds of hours on SPSS, SAS, and R).
2. **Tier 2: Agricultural Extension Workers & Co-operatives (10–15M reach)**
   - Extension agents in Nigeria (AFAAS, ADP), Kenya (KALRO), Ghana, Uganda, Ethiopia act as power users servicing thousands of smallholders each.
3. **Tier 3: Commercial Farmers & Agribusinesses (Agro-dealers, Feedmills, Seed companies)**
   - Variety selection, soil amendment planning, drought risk hedging, feed formulation across Bovine, Poultry, Small Ruminants, and Swine.
4. **Tier 4: National Ministries & International Development (CGIAR, IITA, ILRI, FAO, World Bank, IFPRI)**
   - High-value policy simulation, fiscal budget planning, strategic grain reserve buffer management, and cabinet policy memoranda.

### 3. Monetization Roadmap (Transition from Free Beta to Monetization)
- **Phase 1 (Current - Free Test Running)**: Open community beta for researchers, universities, extension officers, and breeders.
- **Phase 2 (Freemium Researcher / Student Tier - \$5 to \$15/month)**: Free access to basic analysis and knowledge queries; premium subscription for unlimited mixed-effects BLUP runs, automated thesis reviews, exportable R/Python analysis scripts, and full literature syntheses.

## 🏛️ 7 Unified Power Suites (Scientific Architecture)

AgriIntel consolidates agricultural inquiries into **7 cohesive, domain-specific Power Suites** that mirror natural scientific workflows:

1. **🌟 Multi-Agent Orchestrator**
   - Decomposes complex inquiries across 6 autonomous specialist agents (Veterinary, Genetics, Soil, Climate, Economics, Evidence).
   - Features dual-core architecture: live cloud reasoning (Google Gemini 1.5/2.0 with dynamic discovery) with 100% offline calibrated fallback.

2. **🧬 Agricultural Biometrics & Breeding Suite**
   - **Tab 1 (Experiment Designer)**: Generates RCBD, CRD, and Split-Plot layouts; exports custom CSV field books with dynamic crop/livestock phenotypic variables.
   - **Tab 2 (Biometrics & DMRT Engine)**: One-Way and Two-Way ANOVA, Duncan's Multiple Range Test (DMRT), Tukey's HSD, Fisher's LSD, Dunnett's control test, and reproducible R/Python scripts.
   - **Tab 3 (Trait Selection & BLUP)**: Multi-trait radar charts, Smith-Hazel selection index scores, and $G \times E$ stability rankings across 48+ African crops.

3. **🔬 AI Research Scientist & Proposal Studio**
   - **Tab 1 (Proposal Builder)**: Generates 7-part publication-grade grant proposals with Cochran's sample size calculations and 12+ real peer-reviewed APA citations.
   - **Tab 2 (PRISMA Review)**: PRISMA 2020 record screening flows and downloadable evidence extraction matrices.
   - **Tab 3 (Thesis Mentor)**: Socratic thesis tutor and examiner viva defense scripts across 14 agricultural departments.
   - **Tab 4 (Radio Advisory Extension)**: Vernacular audio script generator (Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Amharic) with acoustic marimba chimes.
   - **Universal Multi-Format Exporter**: Instant download in **PDF**, **Microsoft Word (.docx with XML sanitization)**, and **Markdown (.md)**.

4. **🌍 Agro-Ecosystems: Soil & Climate Intelligence**
   - **Tab 1 (Soil Fertility Simulator)**: USDA textural classes, NPK dynamics, pH buffering, and agricultural limestone ($CaCO_3$) requirements for acidic Ultisols/Oxisols.
   - **Tab 2 (Climate Scenarios & Heat Stress)**: Agro-ecological zone mapping, drought return periods, diurnal temperature spikes ($>38^\circ\text{C}$), and seasonal calendars.

5. **🐄 Livestock & Veterinary Intelligence**
   - **Bovine (Dairy & Beef)**: THI heat stress index, SLICK coat hair genetics, trypanotolerance sweeps, and 60-day festive bull fattening financial models.
   - **Poultry & Avian**: Broiler and layer thermal panting calculators, respiratory alkalosis electrolyte therapy, and ND Lasota / Gumboro vaccination calendars.
   - **Small Ruminants (Goats & Sheep)**: Interactive parameter simulators for Sahelian, Red Sokoto, WAD goats, and Yankasa/Balami sheep; ADG sliders, festive ram fattening models (>50% margin), and PPR diagnostics.
   - **Swine Production**: Large White, Landrace, and Indigenous Black Pig interactive controls, wallow/sprinkler cooling guides, and ASF biosecurity quarantine protocols.

6. **🏛️ Agricultural Policy & Economic Simulator (Institutional Macro-Policy Platform)**
   - **Tab 1 (Fiscal Budget Allocation & 4 Ministerial Scorecards)**: ₦5B–₦250B capital allocation across 5 public pillars; models Treasury Ag-GDP/BCR, Labour jobs, Humanitarian poverty lifting/stunting reduction, and Central Bank FX savings.
   - **Tab 2 (Strategic Commodity Portfolio & Import Substitution)**: 12 strategic value chains, land/water demand, FX substitution, and real-time National Self-Sufficiency Progress Gauge.
   - **Tab 3 (Food Inflation & Macro-Shock Resilience)**: Stress-tests 4 global/climate shocks against Strategic Grain Reserve releases, emergency food e-vouchers, and 12-month Food CPI trajectory curves.
   - **Tab 4 (Cabinet Policy Memorandum Generator)**: Synthesizes AU CAADP / Federal Executive Council (FEC) cabinet memos downloadable in PDF, Word (.docx), and Markdown (.md).

7. **🕸️ Knowledge Graph & Developer API Hub**
   - **Tab 1 (Interactive Knowledge Graph)**: Graph visualization connecting crop cultivars, pathogens, soil types, pests, genes, and CGIAR/NARS institutes.
   - **Tab 2 (API Hub & Diagnostics)**: Cloud AI key management (Gemini, OpenAI, Groq), latency diagnostics, and REST API client code.

AgriIntel features a unified document export architecture in `core/exporter.py`:

## 🔬 Statistical Analysis Explainer & Post-Hoc Suite

AgriIntel features a publication-grade biometrical engine that runs:
- **One-Way ANOVA & Two-Way Factorial ANOVA** (with interaction analysis).
- **Duncan's Multiple Range Test (DMRT)**: Stepwise variable range test balancing Type I and Type II errors for agronomic and livestock evaluations.
- **Tukey's Honest Significant Difference (HSD)**: Strict experiment-wise error control across all pairwise comparisons.
- **Fisher's Protected LSD**: Maximum power pairwise $t$-test for pre-planned comparisons when ANOVA is significant.
- **Dunnett's Test**: Specifically compares multiple experimental cultivars/treatments against a single benchmark **Commercial Control variety**, preserving statistical power.
- **Kruskal-Wallis & Dunn's Test**: Distribution-free rank post-hoc tests for non-normal continuous or ordinal data.
- **Genomic BLUP / REML**: Henderson's Mixed Model Equations for estimating breeding values (EBVs) and accuracy.

## 🚀 How to Run the App

### Prerequisites
- Python 3.10 or 3.11 installed.

### Step 1: Open PowerShell / Terminal in Workspace
```powershell
cd "c:\Users\USER\Documents\ML Projects\Agri_Intel"
```

### Step 2: Install Required Dependencies
```powershell
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
```

### Step 3: Launch AgriIntel
```powershell
streamlit run app.py
```

The application will launch automatically in your default browser at:
👉 **`http://localhost:8501`**
